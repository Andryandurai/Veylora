# Prescriptive results: irrigation scheduling

Generated by `python -m analytics.prescriptive.report` (seed 42). **Decision-support estimates under stated assumptions, not field-validated results.**

**Method.** For 20 sampled district-seasons the first 16 weeks of the season are simulated with the daily FAO-56 water balance (`analytics/prescriptive/water_balance.py`) in two weather scenarios (the district-season's typical training year, and the same year with rainfall x0.7). Four strategies are scored by that simulator: `rainfed`; `farmer_baseline` (50 mm every 10 days, as in the plan, 600 mm in 112 days); `even_split` (the budget spread evenly over the weeks, capped); and `optimized` (the weekly LP, `analytics/prescriptive/irrigation_lp.py`). Budget per crop from config/crop_params.yaml (rice 350, cotton 300, wheat 250 mm), weekly cap 80 mm. Cells are the mean over cases with a 95% bootstrap interval (2000 resamples of the cases).

## Solver performance

All 40 district-season-scenario problems solved to optimality; scoring all four strategies for one case (LP solve + simulation + reference scenario) took a mean of 0.08 s and a maximum of 0.10 s (acceptance: LP < 5 s).

## Assumptions and limits (read before quoting any number)

- **Parameters are approximate.** Kc, root depth, depletion fraction, Ky, soil water capacity and prices are rounded values of the right order of magnitude, not table lookups (see the provenance notes in `config/crop_params.yaml` and `config/economics.yaml`). Changing them changes the results.
- **16-week window.** Every crop is simulated for 112 days from the season start with compressed stage lengths; cotton and sugarcane cycles are longer in reality.
- **Rice** is treated as a saturated-soil crop with a small depletion store: ponding, puddling and seepage are not modelled, so absolute rice water use is understated and the 600 mm baseline is not a recommendation.
- **Irrigation depths are net** (efficiency 1.00); each week's irrigation is applied as one event on the first day of the week; rainfall efficiency 0.85.
- **Hargreaves ET0** from district-mean IMD Tmax/Tmin (1-degree grid) and the 'typical year' weather; real fields differ in soil, timing and sowing date (sowing is assumed on the first day of the season).
- **LP vs simulator.** The LP is a weekly linearisation; the table scores its schedule with the daily nonlinear simulator, where it can be worse than an even split in individual cases (counted above).
- **Yield estimate** (when reported): ML yield at normal rainfall x water-stress ratio relative to the farmer baseline at normal rainfall, capped at +50 percent (D-020). The ML model itself explains little beyond yield history (docs/DECISIONS.md D-015).
- **Water saved** is relative to a generous 600 mm baseline defined in the plan; compare also `even_split`, which uses the same budget as `optimized`.
